**Título (28/30):** C2 HELL: Air Defense Command

**Descripción corta (74/80):** Tactical air defense: place radars and missiles, direct fighters, survive.

**Descripción larga (1328/4000):**

You are the air defense controller. Place radars, AA batteries and missiles, scramble fighters and protect your bases as the waves close in. A lite command-and-control sim with NTDS tactical symbols and a command-console look, built for short mobile sessions.

• PREPARATION and OPERATION phases in every level: plan your deployment, then run it in real time.
• Radars, SAMs, SHORAD, interceptors and air assets with range, ammo and rearm modelled.
• Weather, night and electronic warfare that change how you play.
• Game modes: theater levels, daily, endless and more.

HISTORICAL CAMPAIGN: KOREAN WAR (1950–1953)
24 missions of about 5 minutes recreating real episodes: the Hungnam evacuation, the arrival of the MiG-15, "MiG Alley"… Every mission has its own objective (evacuate, destroy targets, shoot down aircraft, keep an asset alive), a period briefing, historical aircraft and units, and an archive of "what really happened". Your pilots get callsigns and kill marks, you earn campaign decorations, and you can share a mission report.

The first 4 missions of the campaign are free. The full campaign and its own upgrades are a one-time purchase; no subscriptions.
Available in English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

A lite simulation: campaigns are representative and educational, not an operational record.
